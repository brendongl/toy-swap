## Extended Pitch (1 minute)

**The Problem:**
You spend hundreds of dollars on toys, but your kid loses interest in weeks. You're stuck with toy clutter, and buying new toys constantly gets expensive. Meanwhile, other parents nearby have the exact same problem—with different toys.

**The Solution:**
ToySwap is a mobile app where you:
1. Browse toys from other parents nearby (Tinder-style swipe)
2. Match when both of you like each other's toys
3. Meet at a local café and swap—no money involved

**Why It Works:**
- Free toy rotation for your kid
- Reduce clutter at home
- Eco-friendly (toys get reused)
- Build community with other parents

**Built for Vietnam:**
Starting in District 2, HCMC. English + Vietnamese support. Safe meetups at public locations.

**We're validating this idea now. Would you use it?**

## Objection Handling (For Comments/Replies)

**Objection 1:** *"I don't trust strangers with toys"*

**Response:**
> "Totally valid concern! That's exactly why we're building trust features: ratings (like Uber), verified profiles, public meetup spots, and in-app chat (no phone numbers). Would those make you feel safer, or is it still a dealbreaker?"

---

**Objection 2:** *"Too much effort. I'll just donate."*

**Response:**
> "Fair point. Donating is easier. But here's the thing: when you donate, you don't get anything back. With swapping, your kid gets a 'new' toy. Is that enough incentive, or still not worth the hassle?"

---

**Objection 3:** *"What if the toy is broken/dirty?"*

**Response:**
> "Great question. We're requiring multiple photos (all angles), condition ratings (Like New/Good/Fair), and you can decline after seeing photos. Plus, 24-hour return window if toy isn't as described. Would that work?"

---

**Objection 4:** *"Not enough users in my area"*

**Response:**
> "You're absolutely right—this only works with critical mass. That's why we're starting District 2 ONLY. Need 200+ users in one area before expanding. If we can't hit that, the app won't work. That's what we're validating now."

---

**Objection 5:** *"I'd rather sell on Chợ Tốt"*

**Response:**
> "Makes sense if you need cash. But selling takes time (photos, pricing, negotiating, payment). Swapping is faster: no pricing, no payment, just exchange. Different use case. Sound right?"

---

**Objection 6:** *"My kid is attached to all their toys"*

**Response:**
> "Haha, fair! Maybe this isn't for you then. But question: Do they actually play with ALL of them? Or are some gathering dust? Those are the swap candidates."
